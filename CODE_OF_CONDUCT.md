# Code of conduct

ContentLens welcomes people who participate in good faith and help build a technically sound, privacy-respecting project.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Give specific, actionable feedback.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities and levels of experience.
- Protect private information disclosed during support or security work.
- Credit other people's work.
- Accept maintainer decisions after the documented process has been followed.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination or sexualized conduct.
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Sharing vulnerability details outside the security process before remediation.
- Spam, deceptive promotion or manipulation of project discussions.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, pull requests, issues, project-managed community spaces and public representation of the project.

## Reporting

For immediate danger, contact the relevant local service first.

For project conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/everton-dgn) and request a private reporting channel. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue.

If the concern involves the project lead or that contact route is unavailable, use [GitHub's abuse reporting channel](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse) or the reporting controls attached to the affected content. This provides an independent route, but GitHub controls its response process.

During the single-maintainer phase, the project has no independent internal adjudicator for a report about the project lead. GitHub may address platform-policy violations, but the project cannot promise an internal remedy until an uninvolved maintainer or a reporter-approved independent reviewer is available.

Reports received through the project route are acknowledged within five business days. The reporter receives a status update within fourteen business days or an explanation that more time is required.

Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a private warning, temporarily restrict participation or permanently ban a participant. Decisions consider impact, pattern, intent, remediation and community safety.

Reports are handled with minimum necessary disclosure. A maintainer involved in a report does not access deliberation or make the final enforcement decision when another uninvolved maintainer is available.

## Appeals

An affected person may request reconsideration with new evidence or a concrete claim that the process was not followed. Repeating the original disagreement without new information is not an appeal.
